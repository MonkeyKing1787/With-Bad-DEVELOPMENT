FAQ

How do I report a bug?

Give us as much information as possible. Include the game you're playing, what happened, what you expected to happen, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots or videos are also useful.

How do I apply to With Bad Development?

Applications are handled through our official applications page. You can access it from the Applications button on the main website.

What games does With Bad Development make?

We currently work on Roblox projects including DOORS With Bad, Scary Baboon With Bad, The Foundation With Bad, and Poppy Playtime With Bad. We also work with other game engines.

Why is a game unfinished or buggy?

Some of our projects are actively being developed. That means you may encounter unfinished features, bugs, balance problems, or things that change between updates.

How do I contact the team?

Use the community channels available to you. For applications, use the official application page. For game bugs, provide detailed information so the development team can investigate the issue.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes. Suggestions are welcome. Explain what you'd like added and, more importantly, why you think it would improve the game or community.
