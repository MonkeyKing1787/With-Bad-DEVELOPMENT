How we got here

Every version changes something. This is where the current build came from.

v0.1
Origin

Monkey Fan Group Bloxburg

Founded by 12aaron1221 as a casual hangout spot with no real projects yet.

v1.0
Rebrand

With Bad STUDIOS

The group became a proper studio and started working on projects including DOORS With Bad and Poppy Playtime With Bad.

v1.5
Breakout

Scary Baboon With Bad

The project brought more attention to the team and became one of its biggest projects.

v2.0
Growth

The team expands

Developers, moderators, testers, composers, artists, and contributors joined the team.

v4.0
Current

Full Recode

The entire website has been recoded from the ground up with a new green terminal-style design, more animations, cleaner pages, and a dedicated help section.
