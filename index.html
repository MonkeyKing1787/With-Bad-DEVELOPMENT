How we got here

Every rebrand is really just the latest entry in a longer build log. Here's ours, version by version.

v0.1
Origin

Monkey Fan Group Bloxburg

Founded by 12aaron1221 as a casual hangout spot — no real projects yet, just a group of people trading ideas for about a year.

v1.0
Rebrand

With Bad STUDIOS

With help from 12aaron1221's sister, the group rebranded into something that actually felt like a studio. The first real dev roster formed: 12aaron1221 (Owner), JustHorror_GAMER — now Ze (Original Co-Owner), Azteco1 and Theshapeshiftsnake (Co-Owners), and PaytonMFriday. The team shipped its first wave of projects, including DOORS With Bad, Ploppy Playtime, and a sequel to each — while UP was scrapped along the way.

v1.5
Breakout

Scary Baboon With Bad

Inspired by the VR game Scary Baboon, this project put the studio on the map and proved what the team could build with a real audience watching.

v2.0
Growth

The team expands

Leadership, developers, moderators, testers, composers, and artists joined in — turning a two-person passion project into a full team spread across three engines.

v3.0
You are here

With Bad DEVELOPMENT

The name changes again — from STUDIOS to DEVELOPMENT — to better fit a team that's building across Roblox, Unity, and Godot, not just one platform.
