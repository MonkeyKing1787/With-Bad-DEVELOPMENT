How we got here

Every version of With Bad Development has been a little different. Here's the build history.

v0.1
Origin

Monkey Fan Group Bloxburg

Founded by 12aaron1221 as a casual hangout spot. No real projects yet, just a group of people trading ideas for about a year.

v1.0
Rebrand

With Bad STUDIOS

With help from 12aaron1221's sister, the group rebranded into something that actually felt like a studio. The first real development roster formed and the team began shipping projects including DOORS With Bad and Poppy Playtime With Bad.

v1.5
Breakout

Scary Baboon With Bad

Inspired by the VR game Scary Baboon, this project put the studio on the map and showed what the team could build with an audience watching.

v2.0
Growth

The team expands

Leadership, developers, moderators, testers, composers, and artists joined in, turning a small passion project into a team working across multiple engines.

v3.0
Rebrand

With Bad DEVELOPMENT

The name changed from STUDIOS to DEVELOPMENT to better fit a team building across Roblox, Unity, and Godot.

v4.0
Current

Full Recode

The website gets completely rebuilt from the ground up. New terminal interface, new animations, new Help Center, cleaner navigation, and a new green development-themed design.
